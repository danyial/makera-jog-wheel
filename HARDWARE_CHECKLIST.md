# Hardware checklist

Only the human ticks items here. Firmware development checks none of this by itself.

## Hardware checks

- [ ] H-1: Confirm the mapping "silkscreen label → GPIO" for all pins from SPEC 7.3 with the diagnostics screen (10.3), before soldering.
- [ ] H-2: Check whether the axis buttons are normally open (both green wires). If normally closed, switch `BUTTON_ACTIVE_LOW` in `core_config.h` and increase the pull-ups on X and Y to 100 kΩ.
- [ ] H-3: Run one button LED directly from 3V3, measure the current and judge the brightness. Up to 12 mA directly on the GPIO; above that AO3401 high-side and `LED_ACTIVE_HIGH = false`. Too dim: `LED_DUTY_PERCENT = 100`.
- [ ] H-4: In the diagnostics screen, exactly one revolution clockwise: counter +400, one detent = 4 counts. Otherwise adjust `COUNTS_PER_DETENT` or `ENC_INVERT` and record it in `DECISIONS.md`. Additionally turn briskly: no lost detents at 3.3 V (otherwise fallback 5 V + BSS138, SPEC 7.2).
- [ ] H-5: Measure the quiescent current after battery shutdown, target under 1 mA.
- [ ] H-6: Confirm the battery divider 1 MΩ/1 MΩ on GPIO34 on the FireBeetle 2 ESP32-UE (diagnostics screen shows mV). If it is missing, add an external 1M/1M divider with 100 nF.
- [ ] H-7: On USB without a battery in J1, read the voltage in the diagnostics screen. It must lie outside 2800–4400 mV or above 3400 mV; otherwise adjust the thresholds and record them in `DECISIONS.md`.
- [ ] H-8: Read the wheel's current at 3.3 V on the bench supply and enter it in `WIRING.md`. Switch the handwheel on ten times at about 3.5 V battery voltage: no reset. Otherwise soft start at the gate (10 kΩ in series, 100 nF gate–source).
- [ ] H-9: On USB with the on/off button off the board sleeps (display dark, no Wi-Fi), with the button on it runs; with the button off the battery charges (record in `WIRING.md`, details H-23 a, b).
- [ ] H-10: Rotary axis A (ticket #14) with the rotary module connected: (a) The status report contains the 4th value in degrees (serial or display). (b) In inch mode (`G20`) the A value stays in degrees, display unchanged. (c) Capture the status report **without** the module (does it still have 5 values?) and check via MDI in Studio what a small `$J A0.010 F0.500` does without the module; result in `DECISIONS.md`. Until then, enable axis A only with the module connected. (d) Confirm pins GPIO36 (A0, button A) and GPIO4 (D12, LED A) in the diagnostics screen (like H-1).
- [ ] H-11: Carrier board (ticket #20) before milling: check the row spacing of the FireBeetle pin headers on the printout (22.50 mm according to DFRobot footprint DFR0654; if different, change `ROW` in `hardware/generate_board.py` and run `hardware/export.sh`). Print `fab/handrad-1to1.pdf` at 100 %, lay the FireBeetle with its socket headers and JST-XH on it: the holes must match. Before inserting the FireBeetle, use the continuity tester on the FireBeetle itself to check that pin 2 of the top row (3V3) and pin 2 of the bottom row (silkscreen RST/3V3/NC/GND: 3V3) are connected. If the bottom pin 2 is VCC/5 V, do not use the board: 5 V would reach GPIO23/19 via the encoder pull-ups. Since ticket #25 the board is in landscape format: the USB-C socket must protrude 1 mm beyond the left edge. Pogo pins P1/P2 (pitch 3.81 mm): check their position on the printout against the pads "Li-ion −/+" on the FireBeetle underside, measure the gap carrier board ↔ FireBeetle underside; solder in P50-J1 with the head at gap + 1.3 mm above the board (jig). After assembly, check with the multimeter: no connection 3V3–GND and BAT+–BAT−; P2 has contact to "Li-ion +", P1 to "Li-ion −"; the PH2.0 socket on the FireBeetle stays empty.

- [ ] H-16: Feed from turning speed and bundling (ticket #32), machine online, axis X, serial monitor running: (a) Turn slowly detent by detent: exactly 0.01 mm per detent, immediate response, no motion without turning. (b) Turn evenly at medium speed (about 1 revolution in 2 s): the machine moves evenly, `Begrenzt` (limited) does not appear or only very briefly. (c) Turn evenly fast (about 2 revolutions per second): no more heavy stop-and-go as before #32; note the impression (smooth / slight jerking / heavy). (d) Measure the overrun after letting go with a stopwatch or the position display. Expectation (SPEC 8.7): distance about `0.08 × turned distance + 0.11 s × speed`, at most 5 mm; time about `0.09 × turning duration + 0.2 s`. Examples: 2 s medium (about 5 mm/s) → about 1.6 mm in 0.4 s; 10 s slow (about 1 mm/s) → about 1 mm in 1.1 s; long fast → 5 mm in about 0.3 s. Never more than 5 mm. (e) The same with XY (diagonal, same feel as X) and Z (overrun at most 1 mm, Z never faster than 5 mm/s). (f) With axis A and rotary module (if available), turn at medium speed: no error, overrun at most 2°; A may still stutter somewhat (the firmware accelerates A itself). (g) Turn quickly back and forth: after letting go, no overrun beyond the window. (h) Turn slow-to-medium for 30 s without pause, then let go: no error message, no position error (end position = display), overrun at most 5 mm (upper limit of the queue). (i) A/B comparison: flash (b), (c) and (d) once with `FEED_LEAD_PCT = 92` (state of the ticket) and once with `FEED_LEAD_PCT = 130` in `core_config.h` and compare (jerking, `Begrenzt`, overrun). Result (in particular (c), (d) and (i)) in `DECISIONS.md`; adjustments of `FEED_LEAD_PCT`, `FEED_WINDOW_MS` or `BUNDLE_MS` as a separate ticket. **Addendum ticket #38:** Until now `F` ran with wrong machine rates (X/Y 2.5 times, Z 1.67 times too fast), so the machine moved faster than turned. Results of (a)–(i) before #38 are not valid; after #38 (rates via `M203.1`, H-20) **re-check everything**, in particular (c) stop-and-go, (d) overrun and (e) Z never faster than 5 mm/s.
