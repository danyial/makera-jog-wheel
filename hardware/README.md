# Carrier board (tickets #20, #25, #49)


The enclosure (ticket #30) is described in [`enclosure/README.md`](enclosure/README.md). The full bill of materials is in [`BOM.md`](BOM.md).

Single-sided, millable board for the handwheel: FireBeetle 2 ESP32-UE in female headers, AO3401, 0805 resistors, JST-XH connectors. Rounded corners (r = 3 mm), **78 × 66 mm**, 4 × M3.

## Layout

- **Landscape (ticket #25):** The FireBeetle sits on top of the board rotated by 90°, the USB-C socket points left and protrudes 1.0 mm beyond the board edge. The former right pin row (VCC … D10, 14 pins) is now the top row, the former left one (RST … RX, 18 pins) the bottom row.
- **Connector rows (rev 2, ticket #49):** upper row (y = 43 mm): J4 display, J6 handwheel (left), J2 on/off button (right end, next to the button in the right enclosure wall). Lower row (y = 54 mm, next to the board edge towards the axis buttons): J9 X, J8 Y, J5 Z, J7 A from left to right, pin 1 at x = 22.0 / 35.5 / 49.0 / 62.5. Battery J1 sits directly above J2 at the right edge (pin 1 at x 66.0, y 31.5): battery and on/off cables both go to the right side of the enclosure, and the battery tracks just run further down the right edge. Left of J2 in the upper row is impossible (the lanes on the right come down there); in the rows the battery lines would have to cross those lanes. Why this way round: the buttons sit just below the board in the enclosure (shortest leads, same order X, Y, Z, A as the buttons); the display cable comes from above; the handwheel cable comes over the battery anyway and reaches the upper row past the button plugs.
- **Topology (crossing-free on one layer):**
  - Battery: `BAT−` (outer) and `BAT+` run from the pogo pins upwards, along the top edge to the right and down the right edge to J1 above J2. `BAT+` is not switched: the battery is always on the FireBeetle's "Li-ion" pads (soft power, SPEC 9.3). The on/off lane passes below J1 into J2.
  - 3V3 rail above the top row; the pull-ups R4/R5 stand vertically right above the pins of buttons Z/A (GPIO35/36, input only).
  - Display (top row pins 2–5) inwards, under the FireBeetle to the left and down the left edge, then right into J4 from above. Next to it on the inside, `ENC_PWR` runs from bottom row pin 6 under the FireBeetle to the left, past RST, and into R6/Q1 from the left.
  - Q1 sits mirrored on the bottom side; its pins there necessarily follow G, S, D clockwise. With D at the bottom, G is on the left and S on the right. That is why `ENC_PWR` must come from the left.
  - Handwheel J6 (0V, A, B, V+): A/B jog left from bottom pins 7/8 into J6 pins 2/3; `ENC_VCC` runs from Q1 below J6 to V+ (pin 4) with R1/R2 below pins 2/3; the wheel's 0V comes from FireBeetle bottom pin 4 (GND) from above (net `GND_ENC`).
  - Buttons X and Y: bottom pins 12/13 (X) and 15/16 (Y) straight down between J6 and J2, then left in the band between the rows into J9/J8 from above (the line from the leftmost pin runs highest).
  - Right group: top pins 6, 7, 10, 11, 12 fan out under the FireBeetle (rightmost pin highest) into lanes right of the rows, inner to outer: LED_Z, BTN_Z, BTN_A, LED_A, PWR_SW. LED_Z/BTN_Z drop into J5 with a 45° jog, BTN_A/LED_A turn right in the band into J7, PWR_SW (outermost) turns right above everything into J2.
  - GND: J4 GND straight down to the bus along the lower edge, the bus to J7 pin 4; J2's GND drops straight onto J7 pin 4; all other button GND pins join the bus from above. GND from the FireBeetle comes via top row pin 3 (display line), the wheel's via bottom pin 4.
- **Two 3V3 and two GND nets:** `+3V3` (top row pin 2: display, button pull-ups) and `+3V3_ENC` (bottom row pin 2: R6, source of Q1); `GND` (top row pin 3: display, buttons, on/off) and `GND_ENC` (bottom row pin 4: wheel 0V). On the FireBeetle they are the same rails. On one layer they cannot be joined without crossing the display lines. That is why they have separate names and the DRC reports no unconnected items.
- **Copper on the bottom only (B.Cu).** Female headers and JST-XH are inserted **from the top** and soldered underneath. The SMD parts (R1, R2, R4–R6, Q1) sit **underneath** on the copper side.
- Pads enlarged for an annular ring ≥ 0.4 mm (no plated through-holes when milling).
- Milling rules: tracks 0.8 mm (supply 1.0 mm), clearance ≥ 0.4 mm (between parallel tracks ≥ 0.5 mm). No vias, no jumpers. No 90° bends: every bend is made of two 45° chamfers (only T junctions stay right-angled).
- M3 mounting holes symmetric, 4.5 mm from each corner.
- The bottom side must be mirrored for milling. In Makera Studio, import the Gerber as bottom layer or use the mirrored SVG. Check the mirroring on a paper printout first.

## Parts

| Ref | Part | Nets |
|---|---|---|
| U1A / U1B | Female header 1×18 (bottom) / 1×14 (top), 2.54 mm (FireBeetle) | see `include/pins.h` |
| Q1 | AO3401 (SOT-23), above J4/J6 | G = ENC_PWR (GPIO18), S = 3V3_ENC, D = ENC_VCC |
| R6 | 100 kΩ 0805, above Q1 | Gate pull-up ENC_PWR → 3V3_ENC |
| R1, R2 | 10 kΩ 0805, vertical below J6 A / B | Pull-ups ENC_A / ENC_B → ENC_VCC (switched wheel supply) |
| R4, R5 | 10 kΩ 0805, vertical above top pins 7 / 10 | Pull-ups BTN_Z / BTN_A → 3V3 (there is no R3) |
| J1 | **JST-PH** 2-pin (2.0 mm), right edge directly above J2 | Battery directly with its PH2.0 plug: 1 (top) = +, 2 = − |
| J2 | XH 2-pin, upper row right end (pin 2 above J7 pin 4) | On/off button (soft power, ticket #49): 1 = `PWR_SW` (GPIO4, internal pull-up), 2 = GND. Only a signal, no battery current |
| P1 | Pogo pin P50-J1 (round head, barrel Ø 0.68 mm, 16 mm, 2.6 mm travel), 0.8 mm hole | Battery − → "Li-ion −" pad on the FireBeetle underside |
| P2 | Pogo pin P50-J1, 0.8 mm hole | Battery + (always connected since ticket #49) → "Li-ion +" pad on the FireBeetle underside |
| J4 | XH 4-pin, upper row left | Display: 3V3, GND, SCL, SDA |
| J6 | XH 4-pin, upper row, right of J4 | Handwheel: 0V, A (GPIO23), B (GPIO19), VCC (switched) |
| J9 | XH 4-pin, lower row 1st | Button X: GND, GND, contact (GPIO13), LED+ (GPIO14) |
| J8 | XH 4-pin, lower row 2nd | Button Y: GND, GND, contact (GPIO26), LED+ (GPIO25) |
| J5 | XH 4-pin, lower row 3rd | Button Z: GND, GND, LED+ (GPIO15), contact (GPIO35) |
| J7 | XH 4-pin, lower row 4th | Button A: contact (GPIO36), LED+ (GPIO12), GND, GND |

Pin 1 of every connector is on the left (top for J1). Connector name (J-number and function, 1.2 mm) and pin names (0.8 mm) are printed on the silkscreen: above the upper row, below the lower row (see `fab/handrad-top.png`). Pin orders differ between the button connectors; follow the labels. Pin orders: J9 and J8 are G, G, SIG, LED; J5 is G, G, LED, SIG; J7 is SIG, LED, G, G. J5 cannot match J8/J9: of the lanes on the right only GPIO15 and GPIO12 can drive an LED (GPIO35/36 are input-only), LED Z must therefore be the inner lane GPIO15, and the lanes land on the connector pins in their order. J7 cannot match either: its last pin must be GND, because J2's GND drops straight onto it (any other GND path would cross the A lines).


Every button connector has two GND pins: one for the second green lead (button contact), one for LED− (black).

**Display pin order:** J4 is 3V3, GND, SCL, SDA. Many SH1106 modules have GND, VCC, SCL, SDA. Wire the cable by the labels on the module, not pin for pin.

**Battery and switch:** J1 is a PH socket, J2 an XH socket. So the battery only fits into J1. The on/off button only switches a signal (soft power, SPEC 9.3): with the button off the handwheel sleeps (about 0.5 mA, H-5) and the battery charges on USB. For storage unplug J1. Without the button cable in J2 the handwheel reads "off" and sleeps.

**Battery to the FireBeetle via pogo pins (P1/P2):** Two spring contacts press from below onto the FireBeetle's "Li-ion +/−" pads. The cable to the PH2.0 socket is not needed. The FireBeetle's PH2.0 socket then stays **empty**, otherwise two sources are in parallel.
- **Mounting the P50-J1:** relaxed head ≈ gap + 1.3 mm (half the travel) above the board's top side. With a calculated 11 mm gap (female header 8.5 mm + pin header spacer 2.5 mm) that is **≈ 12.3 mm**. Hold the pin at height with a 12.3 mm gauge (block or stack of washers) and solder it underneath. About 2 mm then protrude below. Measure the gap first. Do not bend the barrel (0.68 mm); the round head presses point-wise onto the flat pad and scratches through oxide.
- Position: starting point "−" centred between RST and 3V3, "+" level with the top edge of the NC pad (measured). Since ticket #25 each pin sits 0.5 mm further out (`POGO_SPREAD`, user request), centre distance **3.81 mm**, centre unchanged. Across ≈ 2.4 mm from the former right, now top FireBeetle edge (estimated from a photo, the pads are ≈ 3.7 mm wide). Parameters `POGO_*` in `generate_board.py`.
- Coordinates (top view, origin top left, y downwards): P1 (−) x = 12.49, y = 9.70; P2 (+) x = 16.30, y = 9.70.
- **Fallback:** solder a cable to the FireBeetle's PH2.0 plug into P1/P2 instead of pogo pins (P1 = −, P2 = +).
- Before powering up for the first time, check with a multimeter: P2 to pad "+" and P1 to pad "−", then plug in the battery (since ticket #49 J1 is live at once, there is no switch in the battery lead). A short circuit or reverse polarity at the battery is dangerous.

## Known points

- **DRC:** no errors, no unconnected items. Three silkscreen warnings at J1 are cosmetic only: the PH socket's pads are enlarged for milling and cut into their own silkscreen.
- **Header row spacing:** `ROW = 22.5` mm according to the official DFRobot footprint DFR0654 (not 0.9″ = 22.86 mm). Pin 1 (RST) is 11.32 mm below the USB edge. Check against the printout before milling anyway (H-11).
- **Enclosure placeholders (ticket #49):** both connector rows (housings board y 40.7…57.4, below z 22) lie inside `P_JST_rows` of `hardware/enclosure` (board y 39…58, full width); J1 (courtyard board x 62.7…68.3, y 29.0…36.0, below z 22; enclosure x ≈ 66…72, y ≈ 41…48) is no longer inside `P_JST_J1J2` (enclosure x 66…73, y 19…29), which marks its old place. Nothing of the enclosure lies at the new place (FireBeetle and female headers end at enclosure x 63.9, display and its pegs from z 30 up, standoffs at x 77), and the battery cable leaves towards the right wall as before, about 2 cm shorter. The enclosure geometry needs no change; the placeholder should move to y 41…48 at the next enclosure run (not done here: the CAD tool was not available).
- **Antenna:** The u.FL cable leaves at the right end of the FireBeetle. Route the cable to the top right, not over the connectors.

## 3D model

`export.sh` generates `fab/handrad.step` with all parts. The file is in the private repository only (see above) because it embeds the vendor model.

**Original FireBeetle model:** If `hardware/vendor/DFR0654.step` is present (DFRobot/SnapMagic package "DFR0654.zip", unpacked into `hardware/vendor/`; sources and licences in `hardware/vendor/README.md`), `generate_board.py` also writes a temporary board copy `handrad_pcb/handrad_vendor.kicad_pcb` with this model, and `export.sh` exports `fab/handrad.step` from it and deletes it. `handrad.kicad_pcb` and the renders always use the simplified model, so they do not depend on the vendor folder (ticket #50). The folder is committed in the private repository only and left out of the public export (`tools/strip_private.py`): the SnapMagic licence allows use in your own boards, but not redistribution of the model files themselves. Without the folder `handrad.step` contains the simplified model. There are no library models for the FireBeetle and the pogo pins; `models/make_models.py` builds them from boxes (dimensions from photos and the datasheet, good for fit and enclosure planning, not dimensionally exact in every detail).
Heights: female header 8.5 mm + pin header spacer 2.5 mm → FireBeetle underside 11.0 mm above the carrier board, board 1.6 mm, radio module +3.2 mm, PH2.0 +4.5 mm. The pogo pins are shown compressed.
